# THE LEGACY TOUR
### Three Worlds. Three Heroes. One Human Legacy.

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026**

## About the Project

THE LEGACY TOUR is a cinematic, interactive space-journey experience inspired by the challenge:

**"Abandoned but not Forgotten: Storytelling about NASA's Discarded Equipment on the Moon and Mars."**

The project takes visitors on a fictional journey through three chapters of human space exploration:

- **The Moon – Apollo:** Discover the legacy of equipment left behind during the Apollo missions.
- **Mars – Opportunity:** Explore the story of the Opportunity rover and its scientific contributions.
- **Interstellar Space – Voyager 1:** Follow humanity's continuing journey beyond our solar system.

## The Experience

Visitors begin at a futuristic spaceport, register as passengers, receive a digital boarding pass, enter a spacecraft, and experience a cinematic journey through space before returning home.

The experience concludes with a tribute to humanity's spirit of exploration.

## Project Purpose

THE LEGACY TOUR aims to make space exploration history engaging and accessible through immersive storytelling.

It highlights an important idea: even when spacecraft and equipment stop operating or remain behind, their discoveries and contributions continue to shape our understanding of the universe.
